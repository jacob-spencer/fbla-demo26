## Overview

Mainstreet.lt is a local business discovery platform that helps users connect with businesses in their community. The platform enables users to find services, read reviews, and discover exclusive deals from local establishments.

## Features

- **Business Discovery**: Search and find local businesses by service type or category
- **Review System**: Read customer reviews and ratings to make informed decisions
- **Community-Focused**: Highlights businesses in your local area
- **Business Ratings**: View ratings and feedback from other community members
- **Exclusive Deals**: Discover special offers from participating businesses

## Use Cases

- Finding local services and vendors
- Reading community reviews before visiting a business
- Discovering new businesses in your area
- Supporting local establishments
- Accessing exclusive local deals and promotions

## Target Audience

- Local consumers looking for community businesses
- People seeking services in their area
- Community members wanting to support local establishments
- Users interested in discovering deals and promotions

## Tech Stack
- PHP 8.2
- MySQL DB
- Tailwind
- Leaflet.js

## Getting Started

Visit https://mainstreet.lt to:
1. Browse local businesses
2. Search for specific services
3. Read reviews and ratings
4. Discover exclusive deals

## Mission

Mainstreet.lt aims to strengthen local communities by connecting residents with nearby businesses, making it easy to find quality services while supporting the local economy.
